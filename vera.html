Vera

The signal has been received. That does not mean it survives unchanged.

In development

Book 2

Not a repeated motif. A person with pressure of her own.

Vera follows Bardo Conditions, but it does not replay Ben’s book from another camera angle. The recovered material gives Vera her own register: intimate, sharp, dangerous, vulnerable, and resistant to being reduced to symbol or mood.

The bridge begins in ordinary human contact. A diner. Coffee. Music. Cincinnati. A conversation that feels warmer and simpler than the architecture Ben has been using to survive. The horror does not announce itself at the table; it arrives afterward, through interpretation, memory, and what each person carries out of the room.

Continuity

Bardos continues without flattening Vera into Ben.

The governing bridge is love as mechanism and shared frequency without sentimental rescue. Vera can be dangerous without becoming a monster and vulnerable without becoming harmless. Her book holds memory, powerlessness, attraction, withheld signal, and the consequences of being recognized incorrectly.

Bardo Conditions remains Bardos Book 1. Vera is Bardos Book 2. Public cover and release metadata remain controlled until final assets are approved; no generic replacement art is used here.
