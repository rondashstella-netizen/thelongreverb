Bardo Conditions

Grief, signal, recognition, administrative violence, and the unstable border between what was lost and what continues to answer.

Ron Dash Stella · First edition, 2026

A dark literary novel under analog pressure: institutional rooms, damaged authority, tape, static, shortwave language, witness marks, and a signal that refuses to disappear because a system cannot classify it.

The approved publication artwork is used here without replacement imagery. Bardo Conditions begins the Bardos sequence; Vera follows as Book 2.
