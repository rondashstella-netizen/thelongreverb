Bardo Conditions

A literary novel of grief, signal, recognition, institutional power, memory, and the unstable border between what was lost and what continues to answer.

The ledger stays open. The signal continues.

Ron Dash Stella · Bardos Book 1 · Publication candidate / final QA

A valley that records everything. A court that turns concern into jurisdiction. A signal that refuses to die.

A man living between names, memories, and versions of survival begins a ledger: part testimony, part operating manual. The War Court wants every experience converted into a file. The Poor Places want the receiver to go quiet. Against them stand small acts that cannot be processed.

From the valley to Foxtrot City, from the Construct to the studio, Bardo Conditions follows a consciousness learning to separate signal from recruitment, longing from collapse, and survival from transmission.
