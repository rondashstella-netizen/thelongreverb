About the book

Bardo Conditions follows a man who becomes trapped inside a system that records, distorts, and weaponizes his life. As the accusations multiply and the signal breaks down, he discovers a hidden witness layer—others who have been through it, and a deeper record that was never meant to be found.

This is not a story of escape. It is a story of endurance, memory, and the fight to remain human inside a machinery built to misread and destroy.
